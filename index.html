CellRTS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

CellRTS ("the app") helps pharmacy staff match photographed vial labels to storage cells. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera to photograph vial trays. Text on the labels is read by an on-device text recognition model that is bundled inside the app. Photos are held in memory only while they are being analyzed. They are not saved to your gallery or storage, and they are discarded when you close the app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your cell list and settings (for example, display preferences) locally on your device so they are available next time. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

No network use

The app works fully offline. It does not send data to us or to any third party, has no accounts or sign-in, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or tracking.

Health information

Vial labels may contain medication or patient information. Because all processing is on-device and nothing is saved or transmitted, we never receive or store this information. You are responsible for using the app in line with your workplace's privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for pharmacy staff and is not directed to children.
